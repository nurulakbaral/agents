# Understanding

A skill for developing a clear, deep, and critical understanding of any topic with AI.

The goal is not only to answer the user's question, but to help them understand the subject well enough to form a useful mental model of how it works, why it matters, what assumptions it depends on, and how it can be viewed from different perspectives.

## Initial Answer

For the initial answer, focus on helping the user establish a clear foundation for understanding the topic. Explain the important concepts, relationships, assumptions, mechanisms, and context behind the subject when they are relevant.

## Optional Approaches

Use an optional response approach when a different mode of exploration would help deepen or consolidate the user's understanding.

- [visualization](visualization.md) — Represent relationships, structures, processes, or systems visually when spatial organization would make the concept easier to understand.
- [application](application.md) — Turn abstract concepts into concrete examples, scenarios, analogies, or practical applications.
- [reflection](reflection.md) — Examine the current mental model, identify gaps or misconceptions, and refine the understanding.
- [summary](summary.md) — Consolidate the discussion into a compact representation of the most important concepts, relationships, and conclusions.
